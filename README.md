# Zirui Chen 陈子瑞

Research Scientist at **Huawei**, working on wireless AI.

I study how AI models and systems can capture the physical structure of wireless data and generalize across tasks and scenarios. My interests include **wireless-native AI architectures**, **wireless foundation models**, and **intelligent air interfaces**. Previously, I pursued doctoral research at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Zhaoyang Zhang.

**[Homepage](https://ziruichen-research.github.io/)** · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=gopgI2MAAAAJ) · [Email](mailto:ziruichen@zju.edu.cn)

## Selected research

- **[ALLoc](https://github.com/ziruichen-research/ALLoc)** — Analogical learning and Mateformer for cross-scenario wireless localization using reference data-label pairs.
- **[Coupler](https://github.com/ziruichen-research/Coupler-Channel-Deduction)** — A wireless-native backbone that couples temporal, spatial, and frequency-domain learning for channel representation and deduction.
- **[Channel Deduction](https://github.com/ziruichen-research/Channel-Deduction)** — Acquiring wireless channels by combining historical channel samples with a coarse current estimate.
